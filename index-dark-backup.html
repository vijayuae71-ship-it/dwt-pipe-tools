FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DWT Pipe Tools?

DWT Pipe Tools (DWT GmbH, Germany) is a leading manufacturer of premium pipe welding accessories. Their range includes beveling machines, centering clamps, pipe jacks, rollers, purging systems, and more — all German-engineered for precision and durability.

Are DWT tools suitable for field use?

Yes! All DWT products are designed for both shop and field applications. They are portable, robust, and built to withstand demanding industrial environments while maintaining German engineering precision.

What pipe materials can DWT tools work with?

DWT tools are designed for carbon steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, duplex, super duplex, chrome-moly, and other pipe materials commonly used in oil & gas, petrochemical, power generation, and industrial piping.

How do I choose the right pipe clamp?

It depends on your application: Internal Centering Clamps for butt welds, External Centering Clamps for field joints, Chain Clamps for quick setups, Cage Clamps for heavy-duty work, and Reformer Clamps for out-of-round pipes. Use our AI recommendation tool above for personalized guidance!

What is weld purging and why is it important?

Weld purging displaces oxygen from inside a pipe with inert gas (usually argon) before and during welding. This prevents oxidation on the weld root, critical for stainless steel, duplex, and alloy piping to meet quality and code requirements.
